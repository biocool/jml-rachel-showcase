Divorce & family law · By appointment

This is a chapter. Not the whole story.

Ending a marriage is not a battle to win. It is a passage to get through, with your children, your savings, and your dignity intact. We walk it with you, from dusk to morning.

Scroll · one passage, five chapters

Two lives, woven together for years, do not tear apart. Done well, they are untangled, carefully.

Chapter I

Saying it out loud is the hardest part.

The first meeting is unhurried and completely confidential. No decisions are made. You talk, we listen, and by the end you know your options and what each one truly costs.

Chapter II

We protect what matters, in this order.

Before any papers are drafted, we map what your next life needs. Everything else is negotiable. These four things are not.

The children's weeksA schedule they can count on, in both homes, before anything else is discussed.
The homeWhether to keep it, sell it, or trade it, decided with numbers, not emotion.
Retirement & savingsDivided precisely, taxed wisely, so one hard year does not cost you ten.
Your peaceCommunication rules that end the 11pm messages, starting week one.
Chapter III

One life, becoming two. Carefully.

Watch the threads beside you. Nothing is cut. Each strand is followed to its end and handed to the person it belongs to. That is the work, and it is quiet work.

Chapter IV

Most chapters end with a signature, not a verdict.

9 of 10 of our cases settle without a courtroom. The agreement is drafted to be read in five years without wincing, by either of you.

Chapter V

Two households. One childhood. Your next morning.

The paperwork ends. The parenting, the rebuilding, the ordinary Tuesdays, those continue. Our clients leave with a plan for all of it.

How we work

Three promises, kept in every single case.

i.

Children first, always

Every strategy is tested against one question: what does this do to their year? If the answer is bad, we find another way.

ii.

Settle strong, try rarely

We prepare every case as if it will see a judge, which is exactly why almost none of them do.

iii.

Flat, honest fees

A written scope and a number before we begin. The meter never surprises you mid-crisis.
