What is nitro coffee?A simple guide for hosts.

Cold brew with a creamy finish. Here is how it is made, what is in the glass and why guests stop to watch it pour.

Nikki Nitro TeamCoffee & events · Los Angeles

Nitro coffee is cold brew infused with nitrogen. The tiny bubbles give it a smooth, creamy feel and a soft layer of foam on top.

It looks a little like a stout when it pours, but there is no alcohol, no milk and no added sugar. Just coffee, cold water and nitrogen, prepared with care.

01 · The baseIt starts with cold brew

We steep the coffee in cold water for 12 to 24 hours, then strain it. That slow, cold steep is the base for every nitro pour.

02 · The infusionThen we add nitrogen

Next, we add nitrogen to the cold brew. It creates the tiny bubbles that make the coffee feel creamy as you sip.

03 · The pourWatch it come together

As we pour from the tap, bubbles swirl down the glass and settle into a soft layer of foam. Give it a moment, then take a sip.

04 · The kickMore caffeine, less acid

Nitro cold brew carries a stronger kick than a regular cup, which makes it a favorite for long events and late celebrations.

280–300 mgCaffeine per 16 oz of nitro cold brewCompared with about 95–165 mg in a drip coffee.

05 · No extras neededEnjoy it as it comes

Our nitro cold brew is served without added sugar or dairy. The creamy texture comes from nitrogen. If guests want it their way, milk, cream, non-dairy creamers and sweeteners can be added to your event menu.

06 · The showWhy hosts love it

It is visually stunning. Guests stop to watch the cascade, and usually film it. A nitro bar gives people a reason to gather, talk and come back for another pour.

Questions hosts ask us most

Does nitro coffee contain milk?

No. The creamy texture comes from tiny bubbles created when we infuse the cold brew with nitrogen. There is no milk or cream in it.

Guests who like it lighter can add their own milk or cream at the bar.

Can I request custom items for my guests?

Our core drinks are on the menu, but we can offer other tea flavors and add condiments or food items, depending on availability and your event's logistics. Mention it in your booking request and we will confirm what is possible.

How much space do you need for setup?

Very little. Our mobile bars need minimal space and can be set up indoors or outdoors. They come in different sizes and styles, for casual or upscale events and for small or large setups.
