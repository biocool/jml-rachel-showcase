Questions hosts ask us most

Does nitro coffee contain milk?

No. The creamy texture comes from tiny bubbles created when we infuse the cold brew with nitrogen. There is no milk or cream in it.

Guests who like it lighter can add their own milk or cream at the bar.

Can I request custom items for my guests?

Our core drinks are on the menu, but we can offer other tea flavors and add condiments or food items, depending on availability and your event's logistics. Mention it in your booking request and we will confirm what is possible.

How much space do you need for setup?

Very little. Our mobile bars need minimal space and can be set up indoors or outdoors. They come in different sizes and styles, for casual or upscale events and for small or large setups.
