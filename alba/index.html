The Bakery · from 4am

Bread is patience,
made visible.

Our levain is older than the bakery itself — kept alive, fed daily, and folded into loaves that rest for two days before they meet the oven. We stone-mill our own flour and shape everything by hand, in the quiet hours before the city wakes.

Our Story

Begun by a small
coastal kitchen.

ALBA started as a single farm stand on the California coast — a table where growers, bakers, and neighbours first met. We never set out to be a supermarket. We only wanted to keep that table, and set more places at it.

Today the Market, the Bakery, and the Table still share the same shelves and the same belief: simple ingredients, exceptional quality, and moments worth gathering for.

Regenerative soil Zero air-freight Fair grower pricing Compostable packaging

Grow it well, bake it slowly, and share it while the day is still bright.
